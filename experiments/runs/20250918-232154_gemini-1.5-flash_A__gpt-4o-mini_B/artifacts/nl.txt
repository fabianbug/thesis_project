The process begins with receiving an order.  The process ends with closing an order.  An order must be received exactly once. An order must be closed exactly once.  Validating payment is a response to receiving an order. Packing items must follow validating payment. Shipping an order is a response to packing items. Canceling an order is a negative response to packing items. Sending an invoice and delivering an order are both responses to shipping an order.  An order can be canceled at most once. Issuing a refund is a response to canceling an order. Shipping an order is a negative response to canceling an order.  Delivering an order and issuing a refund cannot both occur.
