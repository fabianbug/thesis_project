The process begins by creating a candidate account.

A candidate may create at most one candidate account.

The process ends when the candidate receives an admission notification.

Creating a candidate account is followed by entering the evaluation phase.

Entering the evaluation phase is followed by registering for the selection round.

Registering for the selection round is followed by uploading the admission test score.

Uploading the admission test score is followed by receiving an admission notification.

Registering for the selection round must occur after entering the evaluation phase.

Uploading the admission test score must occur after registering for the selection round.

A candidate will receive at most one rejection notification.

A candidate cannot receive both an admission notification and a rejection notification.
